# product Requirement Document

In this file you will find the problem our product solves, who it is for, and the core features we want it to be capable of

## 1. Product Overview

The product is an AI-integrated ERP interface that enables users to interact with enterprise resource planning systems such as Odoo and SAP through natural language. Instead of manually entering structured data into complex interfaces, users can communicate with an AI assistant like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini (TBD) to describe business transactions conversationally.

The system interprets these inputs and converts them into structured ERP records. For example, a user can input a sentence such as “I sold 500 units of Product X to Acme Corp for $10k,” and the system will process and translate this into a formal transaction within the ERP system. The overall vision of the product is to allow users to “chat with their business,” significantly simplifying interaction with ERP software.


## 2. Problem Statement & Goals

### 2.1 Problem statement
Traditional ERP systems are often difficult and inefficient to use due to their complexity and reliance on structured data entry. Users are required to navigate multiple menus and input fields, which makes the process time-consuming and prone to errors. As a result, data entry is frequently delayed, sometimes by hours or days, which reduces the availability of real-time business insights. Additionally, the complexity of these systems leads to low adoption rates, which in turn negatively impacts data quality and decision-making.   

### 2.2 Goals

The primary goal of this product is to reduce the time required to log transactions by enabling natural language input. 

## 3. Core Features 

### 3.1 Features

 The system must be capable of processing natural language inputs provided through AI assistants and extracting relevant structured data such as customer names, product identifiers, quantities, and pricing information. A central component of the system is the part that is responsible for matching unstructured text inputs, such as “Acme Corp” or “Product X,” to the correct records within the ERP database.

## 4. Users

The primary users of the system include sales representatives, managers, and finance or administrative staff. Sales representatives benefit from the ability to quickly log transactions without navigating complex interfaces, while managers gain access to real-time data that supports faster and more informed decision-making. Finance and administrative users are responsible for reviewing and validating AI-generated entries to ensure accuracy and compliance.
