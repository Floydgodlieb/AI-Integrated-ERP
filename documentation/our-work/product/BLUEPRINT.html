      04 · The safety boundary

      What the AI sends, what the backend adds

      The AI picks one tool and fills in what the user said. Ids, prices and the document type always come from Odoo or from fixed rules in odoo_client.py, whatever the AI sends.

      AI sends

      Names and quantities only

      create_invoice({
  "customer_name": "Pepsi",
  "lines": [
    {"product_name": "Chair",
     "quantity": 3}
  ],
  "invoice_date": null
})

      Also accepted, because llama3.1 really sends them (experiment 13): lines as JSON text, one line without a list, quantity as text, plural names.

      Backend adds

      Checks, ids and prices

      • _map_intent: one of 5 tools; quantity > 0; a date only if it appears in the user's message
      • resolver: "Pepsi" → customer id, "Chair" → product id
      • prepare_review: Odoo list price 50.00, total 150.00, duplicate warning
      • Approval by a human, or nothing happens
      Odoo receives

      A draft, within the allow-list

      account.move.create({
  "partner_id": <id>,
  "move_type": "out_invoice",
  "invoice_line_ids": [[0, 0, {
    "product_id": <id>,
    "quantity": 3}]]
})

      Unknown fields are dropped and reported. price_unit and discount are dropped unless ODOO_ALLOW_AI_PRICES=true. Keys starting with _ are for the card only and are stripped.
