An ERP you can talk to, that never acts without you

A chat assistant for Odoo 20 Community. You type what happened ("invoice Pepsi for 3 chairs"); a local AI turns it into one structured action; the app looks everything up in Odoo, shows you a card with real prices, and creates a draft only when you approve.

Rewritten from the previous group's PRD (documentation/previous-group/product/prd.md) and updated to what V3.1 really does. Every change to this PRD is logged in DECISIONS.md with the reason. How it is built: Blueprint. What comes next: Build plan.

01 · Problem

Why this exists

The problem (from the previous group's PRD)

Small and medium-sized businesses re-type the same business facts into disconnected tools and spreadsheets. That is slow, error-prone and gives poor visibility. They are also risk-averse: they cannot afford long implementations or a system that makes mistakes on its own.

What we focus on first

One high-value, low-risk workflow: turning a sentence into a correct draft quotation or draft customer invoice in Odoo, with a human checking every record. The previous group's PRD calls this the wedge strategy: prove value on a small slice before expanding.

02 · Principles

Three rules, and how v1 keeps them

Assist, not automate blindly

The AI proposes; a person decides.

Nothing is created without clicking Approve draft. Only drafts are made. No confirm, post, update or delete actions.

Integrate, don't replace

The assistant sits on top of the ERP the business already uses.

Odoo stays the system of record. The app only reads and writes through Odoo's own API, as a limited ai_agent user.

Trust first

Show what will happen, flag doubt, keep a record.

Prices come from Odoo, never from the AI. Ambiguous names are asked, not guessed. Every AI call and approval is in the audit log.

06 · Scope

In v1, and deliberately not

in v1
  • Draft quotations (sale.order) and draft customer invoices (account.move, out_invoice)
  • Look up customers, products, quotations, customer invoices (max 50 rows)
  • Ask when a name is ambiguous or missing; suggest one real product for an unknown product name
  • English input; Odoo 20 Community; one PC with a local model
not in v1, by decision
  • Confirming orders, posting invoices, updating or deleting anything: a posted invoice gets a legal number and cannot simply be deleted
  • Prices from the AI (ODOO_ALLOW_AI_PRICES=false)
  • Confidence scores: a small model's self-reported confidence is not reliable (proposal D11)
from the old PRD, later
  • Document or photo → invoice
  • Inventory anomalies, reorder alerts, cash-flow forecasts
  • Other ERPs (SAP, QuickBooks), spreadsheets, two-way sync, data clean-up tools

11 · Risks

What could hurt us

The Odoo user could do more than the app allows. ai_agent is technically allowed to confirm orders and post invoices; only the backend allow-list stops it. Do not add those actions until approvals, audit and duplicate checks are proven.

AI quality is not measured yet. The 64 tests use a scripted AI. Real-model accuracy needs the 50-prompt test set and runs on a fast enough PC (a Mac timed out with llama3.1).

Old secrets. The previous group's V1/V2 contain an Odoo password and a Teams webhook. They must be revoked; never reuse them.

Hardware. llama3.1 needs about 5.3 GB of GPU memory; a cold start takes ~40–45 s. The demo PC must keep the model loaded.
