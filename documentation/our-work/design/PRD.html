PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS · version 1.1 · 23 November 2026 · draft for team and supervisor review

Type what you sold. Check it. Odoo gets a draft.

Sales and admin staff lose time clicking through ERP screens and typing the same details again. With this assistant they describe a sale in plain English, check the customer, products, quantities and Odoo prices, and approve. Only then is a draft created in Odoo. Odoo stays the official record.

1 · YOU TYPE"Create a sales order for Pepsi with 3 Chair"
2 · YOU CHECKPepsi · Chair × 3 at 50.00 · 150.00 before tax · Approve or Reject
3 · ODOO GETSA draft quotation. Nothing is confirmed or posted.

01 · Why

The goal: from minutes to seconds, without losing control

The project charter sets a target: entering a sale should drop from roughly three minutes to thirty seconds. That is a target, not a measurement yet. Reliable matching, visible human control and records you can trace back matter as much as speed.

Typing it into Odoo
~3 min
With the assistant
30 s

Both numbers are the charter's target, not yet measured. They will be measured with the same transactions, entered by hand and with the assistant.

02 · People

Who it is for, and who is responsible for what

The AI and Odoo are tools. People stay responsible: for checking records, for access rights, and for how customer information is handled.

Sales and admin staff

Want simple entry, correct details and errors they understand.

We provide guidance, a readable approval card and a way to correct a request.

Companies and managers

Want time savings, information they can trust, and reasonable costs.

We measure results; they keep the Odoo setup and access rights in order.

Customers

Want correct orders and invoices, and their information handled properly.

Records are checked before saving and errors are corrected quickly.

Partners and suppliers

Want accurate information where it affects them.

The MVP is limited to the agreed sales workflow.

Project team and supervisor

Want a bounded, testable result with evidence anyone can inspect.

Version control, documentation and evaluation records.

Security and compliance

Want controlled access and accountable changes.

Made-up test data only, a restricted Odoo account, traceable actions. Rules for real transactions must be reviewed by the organisation first.

03 · Scope

What the first version does, and what it does not

In
  • Main flow: describe a sale → a draft quotation in Odoo
  • Draft customer invoices (already in the prototype)
  • Looking up customers, products, sales orders and invoices
  • Asking back when a name is unclear, and a review before saving
  • MCP: another AI assistant can use the same workflow (required by the charter, not built yet)
Out
  • Confirming sales orders or posting invoices
  • Deleting records
  • Payments, forecasting, manufacturing and production control
  • A full conversational product
Maybe later
  • The charter allows expense logging as an alternative flow. This plan picks sales and customer invoicing first.
  • Supplier bills need a separately agreed change of scope.

The React chat screen is for development and demonstrations. The charter asks for MCP so that an external AI assistant can use the workflow too.

05 · Rules

The safety rules, in plain words

The AI's output is treated as untrusted input. These rules hold whatever the AI says. Rules 7–9 are new in this version.

1

Odoo sets the prices. The AI never decides money. Letting a user override a price needs its own design and test first.

2

Nothing is saved without approval, and what is saved is a draft. Confirming or posting stays a separate, authorised step in Odoo.

3

Everything is checked: the action, required details, quantities above zero, and which Odoo records may be touched.

4

Names are matched carefully. An exact name wins, then a single partial match. If several match, you choose. A suggested product must be a real one, and you choose it, then approve.

5

Possible duplicates are flagged: same customer, same amount, last 24 hours. You decide whether the repeat is intended.

6

Test data is made up, and the assistant works through a restricted Odoo account (ai_agent).

7

No new customers through the chat. An unknown customer is reported; you create it in Odoo.

8

English only for now. Dutch can come later.

9

Look-ups are limited to customers, products, sales orders and customer invoices, at most 50 rows per answer.

06 · Evaluation

What "more than 90% correct" means in practice

The charter asks for more than 90% of draft sales orders to be correct without edits. We measure that on a fixed set of at least 50 complete sales descriptions: at least 46 must come out right.

46 must be correctat most 4 may be wrong

Two separate sets

Accuracy set: complete, normal sales descriptions. 30 first, then at least 50. This is what the 90% is measured on.

Edge-case set: similar names, typos, unknown names, missing quantities, attempts to sneak in a price, approvals and rejections. These are reported separately and do not count towards the 50.

Anastasia prepares both. Part of the data is kept aside and only used at the end, so the test is fair.

For developers: how it is measured

Precision = correct accepted matches ÷ all accepted matches. Recall = correct accepted matches ÷ all cases where a known match exists; cases that were asked back are reported explicitly.

Record per run: code commit, model, hardware, data version, expected and actual outcome, timing and evidence. Evaluate across input quality and threshold settings. Confidence must come from an evaluated matching method, not from the model rating itself.

Time: measure manual Odoo entry and assisted entry on the same transactions; report cold and warm model separately and say whether review time is included. Repeat the two-session approve/reject test and inspect the saved Odoo records.

07 · Known risks

Two things that could stop us reaching the target

The AI sometimes picks the wrong action

On 16 November the same sentence, "sell 4 premium chairs to Luther", became a sales order once and a customer look-up another time. A fix is ready (pull request #8: the AI answers the same way every time, plus a clearer instruction) but not yet tested with the real model. This is the biggest risk to the 90% result.

Source: experiment 13 in the experiment log.

Speed depends on the computer

The AI model needs a strong graphics card. On the team PC the first answer from a "cold" model takes about 40–45 seconds; kept loaded, 2–3 seconds. A Mac without a suitable graphics card timed out.

RTX 3070 Ti, llama3.1, 5.3 GB of graphics memory (experiments 12, 13). The 30-second target assumes a loaded model.

08 · Definition of done

When is the MVP finished?

Ranked by importance. This ranking is our recommendation and must be confirmed against the charter at the review.

MUST · charter
  • The sales flow with draft controls works (stories R1–R5)
  • Matching is evaluated and reaches more than 90% (R8)
  • Another AI assistant can use it through MCP, and a person still approves every draft (R7)
  • PRD, blueprint, data format, setup guide and demo are reviewed
SHOULD
  • Every draft can be traced back to the request that created it (R6). First step: save the conversation number with each created draft. A store that survives a restart follows.
COULD · before real customer data
  • Log-in for operators, and roles that decide who may approve

10 · Where we are

What already works, and what is still acceptance work

On 16 November Anastasia checked five workflow scenarios in the browser (commit f6bb128, scripted AI, real Odoo). That predates later changes and says nothing about the real AI's accuracy.
